Build Phase 1 of a production-quality full-stack web application called **BidShield** for the hackathon problem statement:

**PS-09-S1 — Detecting Bid Rigging in Public Procurement**

## 1. PROJECT PURPOSE

BidShield is an evidence-first procurement analytics platform that analyzes public procurement/tender data and identifies statistical indicators that may require further investigation for potentially coordinated bidding.

IMPORTANT FRAMING:
The application must NEVER claim that a company or tender has committed bid rigging, corruption, fraud, or collusion. Statistical indicators are screening signals only. The UI must clearly state:

"Screening results identify statistical indicators for further review. They do not establish collusion, wrongdoing, or legal liability."

This limitation must be designed into the application UI, not hidden only in the footer.

Phase 1 is ONLY the application foundation. Do NOT implement the final bid-rigging detection algorithms yet.
